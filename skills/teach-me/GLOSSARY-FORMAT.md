# Course Glossary

For terminology-heavy courses, create `reference/glossary.html` as a reading aid, not a record of demonstrated understanding. For smaller courses, definitions on first use are sufficient.

## Structure

Use the shared course navigation and group terms by technical theme when useful. Give each term a stable anchor, a concise definition, and a link to the chapter section that explains its mechanism. A semantic HTML definition list is a suitable format.

For example, distinguish **concurrency** (overlapping progress of tasks) from **parallelism** (simultaneous execution), and link both terms to the chapter showing their relationship. Keep detailed examples and diagrams in the chapter.

## Terminology rules

- Include important terms introduced by the course during initial generation; no learner response is required.
- Use established technical language consistently across chapters, diagrams, and references.
- Define terms in one or two precise sentences. Expand acronyms and mention accepted aliases when useful.
- Resolve ambiguous or overloaded terminology explicitly, including domain- or platform-specific meanings.
- Distinguish commonly confused terms rather than treating approximate synonyms as interchangeable.
- Keep definitions current when related content changes, preserving existing anchors where possible.
